Acts independently

Within the family’s mandate

Chikitsak handles repetitive, reversible work when the underlying instruction is confirmed.

  • 01Refill the exact prescribed medicine within a preset budget.
  • 02Book the same doctor inside the prescribed follow-up window.
  • 03Track delivery, payment and refunds until the result arrives.
Always asks

When care could materially change

The agent can prepare the decision, but the family or doctor makes it.

  • 01Medicine substitution, dosage change or conflicting instruction.
  • 02A new provider, higher spend or wider information disclosure.
  • 03Anything marked trial, provisional, stale or unclear.

Local-first privacy

The whole record never travels

A small model handles identifiable records locally. Larger services receive only the redacted packet required for one task.

  • On deviceReports, prescriptions, longitudinal history and family permissions.
  • OutsideMedicine, strength, quantity, provider and task-specific authority.
  • NeverThe family’s complete record, unrelated conditions or open-ended consent.
Reports and handovers
